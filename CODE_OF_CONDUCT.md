# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want this project to be useful for security, privacy, engineering, and operations contributors. Please keep discussion factual, respectful, and focused on improving the work.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Explain risks and tradeoffs clearly.
- Challenge claims with evidence.
- Mark uncertainty instead of overstating conclusions.
- Help contributors keep private evidence and sensitive data out of public changes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person or organization's private information.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to expose secrets, customer data, internal policies, or real audit evidence.
- Misrepresenting this project as a certification guarantee.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and related community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also block contributors who repeatedly disregard security, privacy, or respectful collaboration boundaries.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through a private channel when available. Do not include secrets, customer data, or private evidence in a report.
